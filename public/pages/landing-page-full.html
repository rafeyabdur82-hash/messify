Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mess subscriptions, pauses, and safety.

How do meal skips and wallet credits work? ▼

Simply open GharSe before 9:00 AM for lunch or before 5:00 PM for dinner and tap "Skip Meal". Your meal credit is automatically extended to your validity period, ensuring zero financial loss.

Can I pay via UPI apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm? ▼

Yes! We support all major UPI payment apps, QR scanners, and Rupay cards with instant confirmation. You receive a digital pass with a unique QR code directly on your mobile device.

Is the food genuinely 100% Pure Vegetarian and cooked in Groundnut Oil? ▼

Every partnered mess undergoes physical kitchen verification. We strictly verify that only 100% Shuddh groundnut / sunflower cooking oils are used (No Palm Oil) and all premises are purely vegetarian. Strict Jain options are prepared separately.

Can I switch messes during my active monthly subscription? ▼

Yes, our "Flexi-Pass" option lets you switch your dining mess once every 7 days to try different verified kitchens around Rajkot without losing unused pass balances.
